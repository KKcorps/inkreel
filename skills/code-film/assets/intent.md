# Intent

Write this before any code, next to the page as `piece.intent.md`. It is the
contract the whole piece serves. The watch test answers the Judged lines.
`verify.py` checks the finished MP4 against the Measured lines.

## Judged

takeaway: after one watch, a newcomer can say ...
feeling: what they feel when the last frame holds
follow: the one thing we track through every scene, or none, and why
point: the idea or joke that only this subject could give
look: the material, and why it fits this story
text: none | in-world | captions | lyrics, and why
learns from: the gallery films nearest this, and what this does that they do not
ends: how the last frame answers the first

## Measured

length: 30s        # the target, in seconds
pace: brisk        # calm | steady | brisk | frantic
sound: music       # silent | bed | music | leads | follows. music is a composed tune. follows is sound effects on the picture's moments, and rarely what you want
beat: loose        # locked | loose | none
