# Intent

Write this before any drawing, next to the page as `<piece>.intent.md`. It is code-film's card
plus the teaching lines. The watch test answers the Judged and Taught lines. `verify.py` checks
the Measured lines.

## Taught

question: the puzzle the film opens on, in the viewer's words
wrong picture: what a newcomer would guess, which the film proves wrong
takeaway: after one watch, a newcomer can say ...   (two sentences, with the real terms)
real terms: each term the film names, and the picture it names
guess: the question the viewer answers in their head, and what they have seen that lets them
recap: the three or so pieces the recap names, and the frames it reprints
closing line: the last spoken line, which answers the opening

## Judged

feeling: what they feel when the last frame fades
follow: the one thing we track through every scene
look: the material, and why it fits this subject
text: in-world terms and data only. The full words go to the SRT
learns from: the gallery films nearest this, and the approved lesson
ends: recap, payoff, closing line, home chord, the outro and the fade (pace-and-ending.md)

## Measured

length: 100s       # the target. a slower voice with real breaths runs long. cut a scene rather than speed up
pace: calm         # calm | steady | brisk. the approved lesson measured about 0.2 cuts a second
sound: voice       # narration is the clock. the tune plays in the gaps and drops 16 dB under the voice
beat: loose
